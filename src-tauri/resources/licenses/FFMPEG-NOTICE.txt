FFmpeg
------
Connectors ships unmodified copies of the FFmpeg command-line programs
"ffmpeg" and "ffprobe" (FFmpeg 8.1 release series). Connectors runs them as
separate programs; it does not link against FFmpeg libraries.

These binaries are the "lgpl" builds published by the BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds
project and are licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License,
version 3 or later (LGPL-3.0-or-later). They contain no GPL-only or
non-free components. The full license texts are included next to this file
(LGPL-3.0.txt, which incorporates GPL-3.0.txt).

  FFmpeg source code:  https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg  (tag n8.1 and
                       the release-8.1 branch)
  FFmpeg website:      https://ffmpeg.org
  Build scripts used:  https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds

You may replace the bundled programs with your own build: put ffmpeg(.exe)
and ffprobe(.exe) in the Connectors install folder, or point the
FFMPEG_PATH / FFPROBE_PATH environment variables at them.

FFmpeg is a trademark of Fabrice Bellard, originator of the FFmpeg project.
